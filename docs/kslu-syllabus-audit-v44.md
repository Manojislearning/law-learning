# KSLU Semester I — Official-Document Outline Audit (Version 44)

**Reviewed:** 11 October 2026  
**Scope:** 30 imported broad topic outlines, six Semester I courses in `app.js`.  
**Verification level:** `published-outline-compared`. This is **not** confirmation of the scheme applicable to a particular admission cohort, not word-for-word equivalence, and not a statute/case-law or exam-answer audit.

## Primary documents inspected

1. **Karnataka State Law University, revised three-year LL.B. syllabus for the year 2018–19 onwards**. The initial dated university circular is 9 August 2018. Its published Appendix VI-A semester list names the **Indian Penal Code** because it predates the later criminal-law circular.
2. **KSLU circular KSLU/Aca/BOS/2024-25/1586, dated 26 September 2024**, “Introduction of three new Criminal Laws for 3 years and 5 years LL.B Programmes from the Academic Year 2024–25.” Official KSLU PDF: <https://kslu.karnataka.gov.in/storage/pdf-files/Syllabusof3NewCriminalLaws.pdf>. Appendix I gives the five units for **Criminal Law I: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023**, replacing the IPC paper under the published circular.
3. The university’s official **Syllabi** index: <https://kslu.karnataka.gov.in/214/syllabi/en>, which separately links both the original 3-year course syllabus and the three-new-criminal-laws amendment.

The official PDFs were inspected as page images, including original university headings, tables and complete relevant unit-outline pages. The app's summaries were compared for **topic coverage and unit division**, not exact punctuation or prescribed reading lists.

## Specific corrections and cautions

- **English is conditional.** The 2018–19 semester table identifies English as intended **for those writing examinations in Kannada**. The app continues to list its study content for access, but must not describe it as obligatory for all students.
- **BNS replaces the old IPC paper from 2024–25 in the published university circular.** Do not study old IPC section numbers as if they are the new BNS paper's section numbers. The app reflects Appendix I's BNS unit groups but individual law explanations remain subject to current-law checking.
- **Law of Torts Unit V retains the 1986 Consumer Protection Act wording found in the 2018–19 PDF**. The modern Consumer Protection Act, 2019 has replaced the old legislation for ordinary current-law study. Historical curriculum wording and present law are separate.
- **Contract–I Unit V** historically describes the Specific Relief Act as a discretionary remedy and lists an older section range. The Specific Relief (Amendment) Act, 2018 changed material provisions, including the modern structure of Sections 10, 14 and 20. Guided lessons already flag these distinctions; this audit does not certify each example or every subsequent amendment.
- **Admission scheme and later circulars remain uncertain.** The documents reviewed establish what was published for the specified editions. They do not establish whether additional 2025–26 or 2026–27 notices apply to the enrolled student. Do not infer verification from the presence of an official source link.
