# Version 48 — BNS Independent Case Practice

**Reviewed:** 11 October 2026  
**Site:** KSLU first-year LL.B. learning app.  
**Scope:** Fifteen original hypothetical questions (three per published BNS Unit I–V) and reuse of the existing IRAC self-check. These are **not** past KSLU exam questions, official answer keys, legal opinions, verified judicial holdings, or an assessment of a specific admission cohort's examination scheme.

## Primary sources

- [Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 — India Code statutory PDF](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/indiacode/bitstream/123456789/20062/1/a202345.pdf) — offence ingredients, definitions, general exceptions and property offences.
- [KSLU syllabus circular — three new criminal laws, dated 26 September 2024](https://kslu.karnataka.gov.in/storage/pdf-files/Syllabusof3NewCriminalLaws.pdf) — Criminal Law–I (BNS) Appendix I. Broad topic units are compared with this edition, **not** confirmed for every subsequent admission year.
- [Ministry of Home Affairs BNS commencement notification S.O. 850(E), 23 February 2024](https://www.mha.gov.in/sites/default/files/BhartiyaNyayaSanhita_24022024.pdf) — brought the Act into effect on 1 July 2024 except BNS Section 106(2). **A historical exclusion alone does not settle later commencement**; the Section 106(2) question deliberately describes an incident on 10 July 2024.
- [NCRB BNS section index](https://www.cytrain.ncrb.gov.in/staticpage/web_pages/IndexBNS.html) — headings and mapping checks. The enacted statutory wording is the authority for each legal ingredient.

## Learning-app improvement

The app now has three independently selectable original case banks: **Contract–I, Law of Torts and BNS**, or **45 original problems** in total. All use the same study-efficient reader:
- Five varied cases per session, one per syllabus unit, in shuffled order.
- Each unit has three original questions. Completing three sessions *within one subject and open app visit* rotates through all fifteen before repeating.
- Statutory topic and IRAC outline remain hidden until at least a short written attempt. An optional generic IRAC format helps the learner organise facts without revealing the rule.
- A source-linked Issue / Rule / Application / Conclusion outline and a misconception warning appear after writing. Four optional self-check boxes indicate what the student says they included; no misleading machine-graded answer or permanent proficiency score.
- The BNS direct URL is `?v=48&course=bns#practice`. The BNS syllabus page also links straight to this bank.
- Existing profile, review schedule, progress, backups and the other two banks are unchanged. Typed answers are not stored.

## Safeguards and remaining work

- KSLU official document matching proves only the **published outline**, not the academic-year scheme assigned to any individual student.
- Subsection details, defences, evidentiary standards and binding court interpretation can change or depend on the facts; every hypothetical is a teaching abstraction rather than a declaration of criminal guilt.
- BNS 106(2) is **not** categorically labelled uncommenced as of all future dates. The example is explicitly tied to the MHA 2024 notice.
- No claim is made that a student's writing is automatically checked for legal correctness; improvement prompts rely on an honest rubric self-check.
- Browser and Chromium mobile-emulation checks supplement, but do not replace, actual Android Chrome/TalkBack user testing.
